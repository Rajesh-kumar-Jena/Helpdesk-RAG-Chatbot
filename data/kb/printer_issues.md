# Printer Troubleshooting

## Printer shows as offline
1. Confirm the printer is powered on and connected to the same network
   as your computer.
2. On Windows: Settings > Devices > Printers & scanners > select the
   printer > "Open queue" > cancel any stuck jobs.
3. Remove the printer and re-add it using the office printer's network
   name (found on a label on the device).
4. Restart the print spooler service (Windows) or restart your computer
   (Mac) if the queue stays stuck.

## Print jobs stuck in the queue
1. Cancel all pending jobs from the print queue.
2. Restart the print spooler service:
   - Windows: `services.msc` > find "Print Spooler" > Restart.
3. Resend the print job.

## Poor print quality (streaks, faded text)
- Run the built-in cleaning cycle from the printer's control panel or
  driver software.
- Check toner/ink levels; replace if low.
- For streaks specifically, the drum unit may need replacement — contact
  facilities if cleaning doesn't help.

## Physical hardware issues
Paper jams, broken trays, or unusual noises require a facilities ticket
for a technician visit and are outside what this assistant can resolve
remotely.
