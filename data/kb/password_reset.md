# Resetting Your Account Password

## Self-service reset
1. Go to the company login page and click "Forgot password?"
2. Enter your work email address.
3. Check your email for a reset link (valid for 15 minutes). If it doesn't
   arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder.
4. Click the link and choose a new password. Passwords must be at least
   12 characters and include one number and one symbol.
5. Log in with your new password. You'll be asked to re-authenticate on
   any other devices where you're signed in.

## If the reset email never arrives
- Confirm you're using your primary work email, not a personal alias.
- Wait 5 minutes, then request the link again — repeated rapid requests
  are throttled for security.
- Ask your manager to confirm your account isn't locked due to too many
  failed login attempts.

## If your account is locked
Accounts lock automatically after 5 failed login attempts within 10
minutes. Locked accounts unlock automatically after 30 minutes, or an
admin can unlock them immediately from the identity portal.

## Multi-factor authentication (MFA) issues
If you've lost access to your MFA device (phone, authenticator app),
self-service reset cannot restore access — this requires identity
verification by IT security and cannot be resolved by this assistant.
