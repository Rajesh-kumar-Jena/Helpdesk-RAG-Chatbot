# Wi-Fi Connectivity Issues

## Can't see or connect to the office network
1. Confirm Wi-Fi is enabled on your device (not in airplane mode).
2. Forget the network in your Wi-Fi settings, then reconnect and
   re-enter credentials — corrupted saved profiles are a common cause.
3. Restart your device's Wi-Fi adapter, or restart the device entirely.
4. If other people nearby also can't connect, this is likely a broader
   outage, not a per-device issue — check with a colleague first.

## Connected but no internet access
1. Run a speed test to confirm whether it's total loss or just slowness.
2. Toggle Wi-Fi off and on again to force a new IP address lease.
3. Try a different room/floor — you may be near a malfunctioning access
   point.

## Frequent disconnects
- Update your device's Wi-Fi driver/firmware if it hasn't been updated
  recently.
- Disable Wi-Fi power-saving mode in your device's network adapter
  settings, which can cause aggressive sleep behavior.

## Guest network access for visitors
Guest network credentials are available at the front desk and rotate
weekly; this assistant does not have access to current guest passwords.
