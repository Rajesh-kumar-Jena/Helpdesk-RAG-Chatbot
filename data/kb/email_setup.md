# Email Setup and Sync Issues

## Setting up email on a new phone
1. Open the Mail app (or Outlook app) and choose "Add account".
2. Enter your full work email address and password.
3. Approve the MFA prompt sent to your existing authenticator device.
4. Allow a few minutes for initial sync of mail, calendar, and contacts.

## Email not syncing / stuck on "updating"
1. Confirm you have an active internet connection.
2. Remove the account from the Mail app and re-add it.
3. Check your mailbox isn't over its storage quota — syncing stalls when
   full; delete or archive large attachments to free space.

## Not receiving expected emails
- Check the Junk/Spam folder first.
- Ask the sender to confirm they used your correct address — a common
  cause is a typo or an old address from before a name change.
- Check mail flow rules with IT if you suspect an organization-wide
  filtering issue; this requires admin-level investigation.

## Shared mailbox access requests
Access to a shared or delegate mailbox must be approved by the mailbox
owner and provisioned by IT — this assistant cannot grant access
directly.
